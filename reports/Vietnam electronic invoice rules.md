# Vietnam's July 2026 reset redefines e-invoice software

Since **01/07/2026**, Vietnamese e-invoicing has run under **Nghị định 254/2026/NĐ-CP** and **Thông tư 91/2026/TT-BTC**. They replaced NĐ 123/2020, NĐ 70/2025 and TT 32/2025, and the XML and signing standard is now **QĐ 1233/QĐ-CT (27/08/2026)**. For a MISA-style SME app selling goods, the requirements reduce to five. First, issue a **coded invoice (có mã của CQT)** by default. Second, issue it **when ownership passes on each delivery, whether or not the customer has paid**. Third, correct errors **only by notice (Mẫu 04/SS-HĐĐT), adjustment invoice or replacement invoice**; cancellation has been gone since 01/06/2025. Fourth, transmit **through a licensed provider**, because direct connection to the tax authority is reserved for issuers of at least one million invoices a month. Fifth, meet two hard technical deadlines: message version **2.1.1** is the only one accepted from **01/01/2027**, and **XAdES** signatures are the only ones accepted from **10/04/2027**. Fines for late or missing invoices **scale with the number of invoices**, up to 70 and 80 million VND. A systematic timing bug in the software is therefore the largest compliance risk. To meet all this, the developer needs four things: invoices generated from delivery notes, a rules engine keyed to invoice date (old regime, new regime, 8% VAT window, signature standard), a provider-neutral adapter layer, and correction workflows that never offer "hủy". Two caveats run through the whole report. The full text of TT91 could not be retrieved, and the field-level annex of QĐ 1233 could not be parsed. Several details below therefore rest on secondary summaries and are flagged where they matter.

## NĐ 254 and TT 91 replaced the whole 2020–2025 rulebook on 01/07/2026

**NĐ 254/2026/NĐ-CP** was issued on 30/06/2026 and took effect on 01/07/2026. It implements the new **Luật Quản lý thuế 108/2025/QH15** and replaces NĐ 123/2020 as amended by NĐ 70/2025 ([EY Vietnam](https://www.ey.com/vi_vn/technical/tax/tax-and-law-updates/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-va-chung-tu-dien-tu)). Its transitional clause repeals NĐ 123, Điều 1 of NĐ 41/2022 and NĐ 70 ([MISA meInvoice](https://www.meinvoice.vn/tin-tuc/48311/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-thay-the-nghi-dinh-123/)). **TT 91/2026/TT-BTC**, effective the same day, replaces TT 32/2025 ([meInvoice on TT91](https://www.meinvoice.vn/tin-tuc/48237/thong-tu-91-2026-tt-btc/)). The decree itself is slimmer than NĐ 123. It **no longer sets the detailed rules on registration, invoice format, which cases need coded invoices, or adjustment and replacement**. The Ministry of Finance now sets these in TT91 and its appendices ([EY](https://www.ey.com/vi_vn/technical/tax/tax-and-law-updates/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-va-chung-tu-dien-tu)). For developers, the key references are therefore **TT91 Appendix I** (symbols), **Appendix III** (forms 01/ĐKTĐ-HĐĐT, 04/SS-HĐĐT, 01/TH-HĐĐT, 01/BK-ĐCTT) and **Appendix V** (reference display samples). The tax authority explained the changes in Công văn 4831/CT-CS of 15/07/2026 ([meInvoice](https://www.meinvoice.vn/tin-tuc/48311/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-thay-the-nghi-dinh-123/)). One secondary source says a transaction completed in June 2026 stays under NĐ 123/NĐ 70 even if invoiced late in July ([Kế toán Lê Ánh](https://ketoanleanh.edu.vn/kinh-nghiem-ke-toan/thoi-diem-lap-hoa-don-dien-tu.html)). The app should therefore keep a regime switch keyed to transaction and invoice dates rather than hard-coding the new rules. A caution on sources: the NĐ 254 article text quoted here comes from the mirror site hethongphapluat.com, because thuvienphapluat.vn blocked automated access. The TT91 details come from vendor and law-firm summaries, not the gazette.

**Coded invoices are now the default for everyone.** NĐ 254 Điều 6.1(a) requires coded e-invoices from economic organisations, household and individual businesses, and high-risk taxpayers. **Uncoded invoices (không có mã) are confined to listed sectors**: electricity, petroleum, telecom, water, banking, securities, insurance, healthcare, e-commerce, supermarkets, "thương mại" and transport. A firm in these sectors must also have the IT capacity to transmit data, must not be high-risk, and must not have registered for coded invoices ([NĐ 254 Điều 6](https://hethongphapluat.com/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-huong-dan-luat-quan-ly-thue-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-chung-tu-dien-tu/dieu-6)). A trading SME could arguably qualify for uncoded invoices under "thương mại". However, a taxpayer flagged as high-risk on Mẫu 01/TB-KTT must switch to coded invoices within 10 working days ([MAN](https://ketoan.man.net.vn/05-thay-doi-quan-trong-khi-dieu-chinh-hoa-don-dien-tu/)). The app should support both modes but default to coded. **Cash-register invoices (hoá đơn khởi tạo từ máy tính tiền)** cover direct sales to consumers in retail, F&B, hotels, passenger transport, entertainment and personal services. A seller **already registered for coded or uncoded invoices need not register them** (Điều 6.1(c)). These invoices **need no digital signature**, and a buyer's purchase cost backed by one counts as properly invoiced if the invoice can be looked up in the tax system (Điều 8.9) ([NĐ 254 Điều 8](https://hethongphapluat.com/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-huong-dan-luat-quan-ly-thue-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-chung-tu-dien-tu/dieu-8)). Household and individual businesses with annual revenue **above 1 billion VND** must use coded or cash-register invoices (Điều 6.1(d)).

Registration and changes still use **Mẫu 01/ĐKTĐ-HĐĐT**, now under TT91 Art. 6 ([Manabox](https://manaboxvietnam.com/diem-moi-nghi-dinh-ve-hoa-don-2026/)). Every registration change now requires biometric or OTP verification ([einvoice.vn](https://einvoice.vn/tin-tuc/cap-nhat-hoa-don-dien-tu-thang-10-den-12-2026)). The integration architecture is fixed by Điều 16.3(b). **Only issuers of "từ 1.000.000 hóa đơn một tháng trở lên"**, or parent companies with central systems, may connect directly to the tax authority. Everyone else transmits through a contracted e-invoice service provider ([NĐ 254 Điều 16](https://hethongphapluat.com/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-huong-dan-luat-quan-ly-thue-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-chung-tu-dien-tu/dieu-16)). The app never talks to the tax authority (GDT) itself. Paper invoices printed by the tax authority lost validity on 01/07/2026 ([NĐ 254 Điều 44](https://hethongphapluat.com/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-huong-dan-luat-quan-ly-thue-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-chung-tu-dien-tu/dieu-44)).

Điều 8 keeps the familiar invoice kinds. The **hoá đơn GTGT** is for deduction-method VAT payers, including exports. The **hoá đơn bán hàng** is for direct-method payers and for non-tariff-zone entities, with mandatory wording. The **hoá đơn thương mại điện tử** is for exporters that can transmit commercial-invoice data electronically. Exporters that cannot may use a VAT or sales invoice. Điều 8.7 also states that **phiếu xuất kho kiêm vận chuyển nội bộ and phiếu xuất kho hàng gửi bán đại lý are "được đăng ký, sử dụng và quản lý như hóa đơn"**: they are registered, used and managed as invoices ([NĐ 254 Điều 8](https://hethongphapluat.com/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-huong-dan-luat-quan-ly-thue-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-chung-tu-dien-tu/dieu-8)).

Mandatory content (Điều 10.1) covers the invoice name, form and invoice symbols and number, and the seller's name, address and tax code (MST). For the buyer it covers name, address, and **MST, budget-unit code or personal identification number**. It also covers line name, unit, quantity, unit price, pre-tax amount, VAT rate, VAT by rate and totals, the issue date, the signing time, the tax-authority code, and fees, trade discounts and promotions. The buyer's signature is not required unless agreed ([NĐ 254 Điều 10](https://hethongphapluat.com/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-huong-dan-luat-quan-ly-thue-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-chung-tu-dien-tu/dieu-10)). The NĐ 254 Appendix adds the text **"Bán cho người tiêu dùng"** for an individual buyer who gives no details, and a passport number plus nationality for foreign buyers ([meInvoice](https://www.meinvoice.vn/tin-tuc/48311/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-thay-the-nghi-dinh-123/)). One source reports goods naming by the **7-digit product classification of QĐ 43/2018**, or HS code for imports ([Manabox](https://manaboxvietnam.com/diem-moi-nghi-dinh-ve-hoa-don-2026/)). The customer and product masters therefore need fields for MST, budget-unit code, CCCD or personal ID, passport and nationality, a consumer flag, a product classification code, and multiple business-location addresses per MST.

Penalties still sit in NĐ 125/2020. It was amended by **NĐ 310/2025, effective 16/01/2026**, which introduced fines tiered by invoice count. It was amended again by NĐ 291/2026, which adds information-exchange sanctions and shows no change to the invoice fines in the sources read ([meInvoice on NĐ 310](https://www.meinvoice.vn/tin-tuc/42191/nghi-dinh-310-2025-nd-cp-sua-doi-nghi-dinh-125-2020/); [LuatVietnam on NĐ 291](https://luatvietnam.vn/tin-van-ban-moi/da-co-nghi-dinh-291-2026-nd-cp-sua-doi-nghi-dinh-125-2020-nd-cp-xu-phat-vi-pham-hanh-chinh-ve-thue-hoa-don-186-110744-article.html)). The table shows the tiers for ordinary sales ([MISA AMIS on NĐ 310](https://amis.misa.vn/256903/nghi-dinh-310-2025-nd-cp-ve-xu-phat-vi-pham-hanh-chinh-ve-thue-hoa-don/)). A lighter table applies to promotions, gifts, samples, internal use and goods loans. It starts at a warning for one invoice and tops out at 15–30M VND (late) or 30–50M VND (not issued) for 100 or more invoices. NĐ 254 Điều 41 also pays whistleblowing consumers up to **10% of the fine, capped at 10M VND** per case ([meInvoice](https://www.meinvoice.vn/tin-tuc/48311/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-thay-the-nghi-dinh-123/)).

| Invoices affected | Issued at wrong time (VND) | Not issued (VND) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5–1.5M | 1–2M |
| 2 to under 10 | 2–5M | 2–10M |
| 10 to under 20 | 5–15M | 10–30M |
| 20 to under 50 | 15–30M | 30–50M |
| 50 to under 100 | 30–50M | 60–80M |
| 100 or more | 50–70M | 60–80M |

## QĐ 1233 sets two cut-overs: version 2.1.1 on 01/01/2027 and XAdES on 10/04/2027

The XML standard has changed three times. QĐ 1450/QĐ-TCT (2021), amended by 1510 (2022), was replaced by **QĐ 1306/QĐ-CT on 13/06/2025** ([nhansu.vn](https://nhansu.vn/van-ban-phap-luat/Quyet-dinh-1306-QD-CT-2025-thanh-phan-chua-du-lieu-nghiep-vu-hoa-don-chung-tu-dien-tu-664897.html)). That was replaced by **QĐ 1233/QĐ-CT, dated 27/08/2026** and effective on signing. Under its Article 2, message versions **2.1.0 and 2.1.1** run in parallel only until **31/12/2026**. Signature standards **DSig and XAdES** run in parallel only until **09/04/2027** ([LuatVietnam on 1233](https://luatvietnam.vn/thue/quyet-dinh-1233-qd-ct-2026-quy-dinh-thanh-phan-du-lieu-hoa-don-chung-tu-dien-tu-446016-d1.html)). Công văn 7714/CT-CĐS (16/10/2026) scheduled XAdES rollout for **November 2026**, and the tax system began receiving QĐ 1233 messages that month ([einvoice.vn](https://einvoice.vn/tin-tuc/cap-nhat-hoa-don-dien-tu-thang-10-den-12-2026); [Thuế & Hải quan](https://thuehaiquan.tapchikinhtetaichinh.vn/nang-cap-ha-tang-cong-nghe-thong-tin-phuc-vu-trien-khai-hoa-don-chung-tu-dien-tu-170047.html)). The general rules are: numbers of at most 21 digits with up to 6 decimal places, dates as `YYYY-MM-DD`, and date-times in GMT+7 ([M-Invoice](https://minvoice.vn/quyet-dinh-1233-qd-ct-quy-dinh-moi-ve-du-lieu-hoa-don-chung-tu-dien-tu/)). The 1233 annex PDF exceeds 10 MB and could not be parsed. **The field-level differences of version 2.1.1 are therefore unverified**, including any new buyer-ID tags for CCCD, passport or nationality. The structure below comes from QĐ 1450. The 1233 annexes use the same tag families, so it very likely carries over.

The invoice is `HDon > DLHDon (TTChung, NDHDon (NBan, NMua, DSHHDVu, TToan)) + DLQRCode + MCCQT + DSCKS`. `TTChung` carries the following fields:

- PBan, the message version;
- KHMSHDon, the one-digit form number;
- KHHDon, the 6-character symbol;
- SHDon, the invoice number, up to 8 digits;
- NLap, the issue date;
- **DVTTe**, a 3-letter currency code;
- **TGia**, the exchange rate, Number(7,2), required unless the currency is VND;
- HTTToan, the payment method, free text;
- for corrections, the **TTHDLQuan** block.

Line items carry the fields TChat, MHHDVu, THHDVu, DVTinh, SLuong, DGia, TLCKhau, STCKhau, ThTien and TSuat. Totals are broken down per rate, followed by TgTCThue, TgTThue, TTCKTMai (total trade discount), TgTTTBSo and TgTTTBChu (amount in words). The issuer never fills **MCCQT**. The tax system generates it and inserts it into `HDon`: a unique string of up to 34 characters ([LuatVietnam on 1450](https://luatvietnam.vn/thue/quyet-dinh-1450-qd-tct-bo-tai-chinh-210675-d1.html)). The code lists in the 1233 annexes that the app must model are below ([LuatVietnam on 1233](https://luatvietnam.vn/thue/quyet-dinh-1233-qd-ct-2026-quy-dinh-thanh-phan-du-lieu-hoa-don-chung-tu-dien-tu-446016-d1.html)).

| Field | Values (QĐ 1233) | Build note |
|---|---|---|
| TChat (line nature) | 1 goods/service, 2 promotion, 3 trade discount, 4 note, 5 specialty goods | Drives promotion and discount lines |
| TSuat (VAT rate) | 0%, 5%, 8%, 10%, KCT, KKKNT, KHAC or KHAC:AB.CD%, CTTC, KTPVDC, KTGTT | Store as a string code, never a number |
| DVTTe (currency) | 21 ISO codes: VND, USD, EUR, JPY, CNY, KRW, SGD, THB and others | TGia required when not VND |
| TCHDon / LHDCLQuan | 1 replace, 2 adjust / related invoice 1 = NĐ123/70, **5 = NĐ254** | Choose by the original invoice's date |
| Adjustment reason (Annex XVII) | 4 trade discount, 5 return of unregistered goods, 8 cancelled transaction, 13 general error, 14 replace cash-register invoice, among 14 codes | New coded reason field on corrections |

The form number is one digit from **1 to 9**: 1 VAT, 2 sales, 6 internal-transfer or consignment note, 7 hoá đơn thương mại điện tử, 8–9 invoices integrated with receipts. The symbol has **6 characters**. The first is **C** (coded) or **K** (uncoded). The next two are the year. The fourth is a type letter: **T** registered enterprise, **M** cash register, **N** internal-transfer note, **B** consignment note, **X** hoá đơn thương mại điện tử. The last two are seller-chosen. Examples are 1C26TAA and 6K26NAB ([einvoice.vn](https://einvoice.vn/tin-tuc/quy-dinh-ve-ky-hieu-mau-so-ky-hieu-hoa-don-dien-tu-tu-ngay-01-6-2025-theo-thong-tu-32-2025-tt-btc)). MISA reports that TT91 adds **F**, for a VAT invoice combined with a refund declaration ([MISA on TT91](https://amis.misa.vn/279873/thong-tu-91-2026-tt-btc/)). Whether X is new or carried over is disputed between sources. Numbers run sequentially per symbol and **restart at 1 every year** ([MAN](https://thue.man.net.vn/nghi-dinh-254-2026/)). The app must therefore roll series on 1 January: an invoice dated in 2027 cannot carry a "26" symbol.

Every payload travels in a `TDiep` envelope whose `MLTDiep` code names the message. The main codes are:

- 200: send for a code;
- 202: code result;
- 203: uncoded data;
- 204: check result (01/TB-KTDL);
- 206 / 216: coded / uncoded cash-register invoice;
- 208 / 210: adjustment or replacement of many invoices with a bảng kê;
- 300: error notice;
- 400: summary table;
- 999: technical acknowledgement;
- -1: format error.

([LuatVietnam on 1233](https://luatvietnam.vn/thue/quyet-dinh-1233-qd-ct-2026-quy-dinh-thanh-phan-du-lieu-hoa-don-chung-tu-dien-tu-446016-d1.html)). In practice, a coded invoice is legally complete only when the 202 response returns the invoice with MCCQT and the tax authority's signature. "Signed by the seller" and "coded by the tax authority" must therefore be separate states in the app.

On uncoded invoices, the default for goods sellers is to send the full content to the buyer and the tax authority **no later than the next working day** after issuance (Điều 16.3(a.3)). Summary tables (01/TH-HĐĐT) are reserved for listed sectors ([NĐ 254 Điều 16](https://hethongphapluat.com/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-huong-dan-luat-quan-ly-thue-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-chung-tu-dien-tu/dieu-16)). The seller signs `DLHDon` with XML Signature, placed in `DSCKS/NBan` with a `SigningTime` property and the embedded certificate. The tax authority countersigns in `DSCKS/CQT` ([LuatVietnam on 1450](https://luatvietnam.vn/thue/quyet-dinh-1450-qd-tct-bo-tai-chinh-210675-d1.html)). If the signing time differs from the issue date, the data must still go out no later than the next working day ([MAN](https://thue.man.net.vn/nghi-dinh-254-2026/)). **The exact XAdES profile, including any timestamping requirement, could not be confirmed from any primary source.** A QR block (`DLQRCode`) is optional and follows the EMVCo/VietQR layout with an invoice-information sub-field.

The reduced **8% VAT rate** under Nghị quyết 204/2025/QH15 and NĐ 174/2025 runs only to **31/12/2026**, with listed exclusions. No extension had been found as of 11/11/2026 ([einvoice.vn on NĐ 174](https://einvoice.vn/van-ban-hddt/nghi-dinh-174-2025-nd-cp-quy-dinh-ve-chinh-sach-giam-thue-gtgt-theo-nghi-quyet-so-204-2025-qh15)). The app should therefore gate the 8% option by invoice date.

## Only MISA documents its API publicly, so build a provider-neutral adapter

Because SMEs must transmit through a provider, the integration surface is the provider's API, not the tax authority's XML. The main providers are Viettel S-Invoice, VNPT Invoice, MISA meInvoice, BKAV eHoadon, Softdreams EasyInvoice, M-Invoice, Thái Sơn, FPT and MobiFone. POS platforms such as KiotViet, Sapo, Haravan and iPOS now appear on the tax authority's 2026 list of 39 technology providers for household businesses ([Thuế & Hải quan](https://thuehaiquan.tapchikinhtetaichinh.vn/cong-bo-39-ong-lon-cong-nghe-tiep-suc-ho-kinh-doanh-trien-khai-hoa-don-dien-tu-152563.html)). **No credible market-share data exists**, only vendor "number one" claims. The customer, not the developer, usually chooses the provider. That is the decisive argument for a multi-provider design.

Public documentation is thin. **MISA meInvoice** is the only provider with a developer site (doc.meinvoice.vn), which describes REST/JSON integration ([meInvoice](https://www.meinvoice.vn/?p=44747)). The community .NET SDK MisaConnect.EInvoice (v2.10.0, 07/11/2026) confirms the sandbox at `https://testapi.meinvoice.vn/api/integration` and production at `https://api.meinvoice.vn/api/integration`. It authenticates with TaxCode, UserName, Password and AppId to obtain a Bearer token. Its operations include templates, PDF preview, draft save, lookup by RefId, adjustment and replacement ([NuGet](https://www.nuget.org/packages/MisaConnect.EInvoice)). This is community code, not an official MISA product. **Viettel S-Invoice** circulates a V2.0 webservice spec (11/2024) outside its own domain. Viindoo's guide gives the v2 base `https://api-vinvoice.viettel.vn/services/einvoiceapplication/api/` with username/password authentication and **server-side signing**. It also notes that 04/SS error notices must be filed on the S-Invoice portal, not through the API ([Viindoo](https://viindoo.com/documentation/15.0/applications/finance/accounting-and-invoicing/fiscal-localizations/issue-e-invoice-by-integrating-viindoo-with-viettel-s-s-invoice-service.html)). **VNPT, BKAV, EasyInvoice, M-Invoice, FPT, CyberLotus and Thái Sơn publish no API reference.** Their specs come from account managers. VNPT's SmartCA partner portal points to remote signing as its route ([VNPT SmartCA](https://doitac-smartca.vnpt.vn/help/document/TL_tich_hop_ban_hang_SmartCA_v1.0.pdf)). Recalled details for these providers, such as Viettel paths with Basic auth and `transactionUuid`, a VNPT SOAP service with `Fkey`, and a BKAV `ExecCommand` endpoint, were not verified and must be confirmed from the specs the providers supply.

Two aggregators put one API in front of several providers. **SePay** uses Bearer tokens, and its create, issue, adjust and replace calls are asynchronous: each returns a `tracking_code` to poll. It documents no webhooks and no cancel endpoint ([SePay](https://developer.sepay.vn/en/einvoice-api/tong-quan)). **Tingee** signs requests with HMAC-SHA512. It takes a partner-generated `invoiceCode` as the idempotency key and carries currency, exchange rate, percentage or amount discounts, and tax codes -1 (KCT) and -2 (KKKNT) ([Tingee](http://developers.tingee.vn/docs/hoa-don-dien-tu/create/)). The best reference architecture is Viindoo's Odoo 18 module `l10n_vn_viin_edi`. It is a provider-neutral base with a normalised status model (to issue, issued, adjusted, replaced, cancelled, error) and per-provider adapters for S-Invoice, VN-Invoice and meInvoice. By default it **blocks cancelling issued invoices from 01/07/2026**. It also shows real capability gaps: increasing adjustments are not available through the API for MISA or VN-Invoice in that module ([Viindoo app store](https://viindoo.com/vi/apps/app/18.0/l10n_vn_viin_edi)). Prices fell sharply in 2025. Viettel, VNPT and MobiFone committed to under 50 VND per invoice for household businesses, and M-Invoice and Fast to 30 VND ([VCCI](https://www.vcci.com.vn/tin-tuc/nha-cung-cap-dich-vu-cam-ket-giam-gia-hoa-don-dien-tu-cho-ho-kinh-doanh)).

The integration design follows from these facts. Define an `EInvoiceProvider` interface with these operations: list templates and series, create draft, issue, adjust, replace, get status, get PDF/XML, email buyer, and send 04/SS where supported. Keep a per-tenant provider configuration and a **capability matrix** for each provider. Before every call, generate and persist a partner key (RefId, transactionUuid, invoiceCode or Fkey). After a timeout, look the invoice up by that key and never blindly re-create it. Poll in a background job until the MCCQT arrives, then fetch the final XML and PDF. Require HSM or remote-signing certificates rather than USB-token plug-ins, which suit desktop clients, not a web app. Before 01/01/2027, confirm in writing that each supported provider has moved to QĐ 1233 / 2.1.1 and XAdES. Any API document dated before mid-2026 is suspect.

## Delivery, not the order or the payment, triggers the invoice

NĐ 254 Điều 9.1 fixes the invoice moment for goods. It is the transfer of ownership or right of use **"không phân biệt đã thu được tiền hay chưa thu được tiền"**, whether or not money has been collected. Exports must be invoiced **no later than the next working day after customs clearance**. Điều 9.3 requires that **"mỗi lần giao hàng"**, each delivery, be invoiced for the quantity delivered ([NĐ 254 Điều 9](https://hethongphapluat.com/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-huong-dan-luat-quan-ly-thue-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-chung-tu-dien-tu/dieu-9)). It follows that quotations and undelivered sales orders never produce invoices. The natural anchor for an invoice is the delivery note (phiếu xuất kho bán hàng), not the sales order. The table maps each scenario to the current rule. The business-scenario research cited NĐ 123/NĐ 70/TT 32. Where those rules carried over, the current instrument is cited. Where NĐ 254 changed them, the table says so.

| Scenario | Invoice? | Document and timing | Current basis | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quotation; confirmed but undelivered sales order | No | None until delivery | NĐ 254 Điều 9.1 (derived) | High |

**Returns and discounts.** For returns, the seller is now the default issuer. It issues an adjustment invoice referencing the original, with reason code 5 (return of unregistered goods). The buyer may issue a return invoice instead if the parties agree. For registered assets such as vehicles, the buyer issues it ([MAN on TT91](https://thue.man.net.vn/thong-tu-91-2026/)). This reverses older pre-NĐ 70 practice, in which the buyer usually invoiced the return. The rule carried over from the NĐ 70 amendment of Điều 19 ([LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/thue-phi-le-phi/cach-xu-ly-hoa-don-dien-tu-khi-tra-lai-hang-hoa-565-101871-article.html)). The sources draw no business-versus-consumer distinction. Consumers cannot issue invoices anyway, so the seller's decrease adjustment is their only route, and a full return becomes an adjustment down to zero. The return voucher should require a return minute (biên bản trả hàng) as an attachment. Period-end volume discounts follow TT91 Art. 10. The seller either deducts the discount on the last or a later invoice, without exceeding that invoice's value, or issues a separate adjustment invoice with a list ([MAN on TT91](https://thue.man.net.vn/thong-tu-91-2026/)). MISA's worked example shows the overflow case: a 30M VND discount against a 10M VND final invoice ([MISA AMIS](https://amis.misa.vn/40617/hoa-don-chiet-khau-thuong-mai/)). Early-payment discounts are financial items (TK 635 for the seller, TK 515 for the buyer) and **must not be invoiced** ([MISA AMIS](https://amis.misa.vn/26292/hach-toan-chiet-khau-thanh-toan/)).

**Promotions.** Treating free goods as "price zero, so no invoice" is a violation ([meInvoice](https://www.meinvoice.vn/?p=38685)). The VAT taxable price of goods promoted under commercial law is zero, while discount-type promotions are taxed at the reduced price. Promotions run outside commercial-law rules are taxed like gifts, at market value ([MOF Q&A](https://portal.mof.gov.vn/hoidapcstc/home/cthoidap/158126)). NĐ 254 adds an option the business-scenario research could not confirm under NĐ 70: **promotional and gift goods may be invoiced in a monthly or quarterly summary invoice matching the VAT return period** ([EY](https://www.ey.com/vi_vn/technical/tax/tax-and-law-updates/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-va-chung-tu-dien-tu)). The app should keep a promotion-programme master with its registration reference and validity dates. It should emit TChat 2 lines at zero price, and warn when a free line falls outside a valid programme.

**Exports and foreign currency.** The hoá đơn thương mại điện tử, which the business-scenario notes could not confirm, is now explicit in Điều 8.3 as form 7, letter X. It is limited to exporters that transmit commercial-invoice data electronically. Others keep using a VAT invoice ([NĐ 254 Điều 8](https://hethongphapluat.com/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-huong-dan-luat-quan-ly-thue-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-chung-tu-dien-tu/dieu-8)). Customs filings use the commercial invoice and packing list, and the e-invoice follows clearance ([MISA AMIS](https://amis.misa.vn/89381/hoa-don-dien-tu-xuat-khau/)). The 0% rate requires an export contract, non-cash payment documents and customs evidence under NĐ 181/2025 and TT 69/2025 ([Lao Động](https://news.laodong.vn/kinh-doanh/dieu-kien-ap-dung-thue-suat-gia-tri-gia-tang-0-cho-hang-hoa-dich-vu-xuat-khau-tu-17-1533622.ldo)). In the XML, the currency goes in DVTTe from the 21-code list, with TGia mandatory. Amounts carry up to 6 decimals. **The rule for which exchange rate to use, and how to round the VND equivalent, sits in point 8 of the NĐ 254 Appendix, which was not retrieved.** Until it is read, store the original-currency amounts at source precision, the rate, and a VND equivalent rounded to whole đồng, and follow the provider's convention. The app should date the invoice from the clearance date on the customs declaration and alert the user at clearance plus one working day.

**Retail counter and buyer identity.** Every retail sale is invoiced, even when the customer declines an invoice ([LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/thue-phi-le-phi/xuat-hoa-don-cho-khach-hang-ca-nhan-khong-lay-hoa-don-nhu-the-nao-565-102155-article.html)). The buyer line reads "Bán cho người tiêu dùng" when no details are given, and such invoices cannot support the buyer's expense claims ([Manabox](https://manaboxvietnam.com/diem-moi-nghi-dinh-ve-hoa-don-2026/)). A buyer with a business registration must get full details. A tax office warned in Công văn 2921/ĐTTNG-QLDN against writing "khách lẻ không lấy hóa đơn" for such buyers ([Kế toán Lê Ánh](https://ketoanleanh.edu.vn/kinh-nghiem-ke-toan/cong-van-2921dttng-qldn-huong-dan-nnt-lap-hoa-don-dien-tu-theo-quy-dinh-tai-nghi-dinh-702025nd-cp.html)). An SME already on coded invoices can issue them at the counter, or optionally register a cash-register (M) series for speed. Cash-register invoices need no signature, but errors on them can only be fixed by replacement. Whether ordinary retail may consolidate a day's sales into one invoice was contested under NĐ 70: Sapo said no, while a thuvienphapluat Q&A and a Grant Thornton newsletter described the practice ([Sapo](https://www.sapo.vn/blog/giai-dap-chi-tiet-nd70-2025-nd-cp-phan-2); [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.com.vn/contentassets/0a95b00a770a4a50abe80985fe2502dd/tax-newsletter-june-2025---vietnamese-version.pdf)). NĐ 254 Điều 9.4 names end-of-day summaries only for hospitals and specific services, so **one invoice per POS transaction is the defensible design**. Điều 9.5 adds one relief: a seller without automatic invoicing software may issue night-shift sales by the next working day ([NĐ 254 Điều 9](https://hethongphapluat.com/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-huong-dan-luat-quan-ly-thue-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-chung-tu-dien-tu/dieu-9)).

**Internal flows, production and invoice-less purchases.** Transfers between the company's own warehouses or branches use the phiếu xuất kho kiêm vận chuyển nội bộ (form 6, letter N), and goods consigned to agents use the consignment delivery note (letter B). Both are registered and numbered like invoices but are not VAT invoices. Điều 7 lists eight cases needing no e-invoice, including capital contributions in kind, asset transfers between parent and dependent units, and internal production use as defined in VAT decree NĐ 181/2025 ([meInvoice](https://www.meinvoice.vn/tin-tuc/48311/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-thay-the-nghi-dinh-123/)). Issuing materials to a production order and receiving finished goods therefore produce no invoice. **A tension remains**: the NĐ 310 penalty table still names "tiêu dùng nội bộ" (internal use) as an invoiceable flow ([MISA AMIS](https://amis.misa.vn/256903/nghi-dinh-310-2025-nd-cp-ve-xu-phat-vi-pham-hanh-chinh-ve-thue-hoa-don/)). Internal consumption outside production, such as goods taken for office use, should keep an invoice option until Điều 7 is read in full. Purchases from households and individuals without invoices are documented on **Bảng kê 02/TNDN** under TT 20/2026 and NĐ 320/2025 ([MISA SME](https://sme.misa.vn/349797/bang-ke-02-tndn/)). The form is allowed only for these cases: farm and aquatic produce from the producer, handicrafts, scrap from collectors, personal property, and sellers with revenue under 1 billion VND a year. Payments of 5M VND or more to one seller on one day must be non-cash. The effective date of that condition is reported as 01/10/2025 by some sources and 15/12/2025 by another ([einvoice.vn](https://einvoice.vn/tin-tuc/mua-hang-khong-co-hoa-don); [meInvoice](https://www.meinvoice.vn/?p=51891)). Because households above the threshold must now invoice, the purchase module should enforce the allowed categories, capture the seller's CCCD, set input VAT to non-deductible, and warn on cash payments of 5M VND or more.

## Corrections run only through notice, adjustment or replacement

Cancelling (hủy) an erroneous e-invoice has been impossible since NĐ 70 took effect on 01/06/2025 ([Sapo](https://www.sapo.vn/blog/dieu-kien-va-thu-tuc-huy-hoa-don-dien-tu)). TT91 Art. 10 now offers three routes. The first is for errors limited to the buyer's name or address, and per some sources the amount in words, where MST, amounts, rate, tax and goods are correct. The seller notifies the buyer and files **Mẫu 04/SS-HĐĐT**, with no new invoice. The sources disagree on whether "amount in words" is inside this route ([MAN](https://ketoan.man.net.vn/05-thay-doi-quan-trong-khi-dieu-chinh-hoa-don-dien-tu/); [Arito](https://arito.vn/en/thoi-han-nop-mau-04-ss-hddt-la-khi-nao/)). The second and third routes cover errors in MST, goods, quality, amount, rate or tax. Here the seller **chooses either an adjustment invoice ("Điều chỉnh cho hóa đơn Mẫu số… ký hiệu… số… ngày…") or a replacement invoice ("Thay thế cho hóa đơn…")**. A replacement is sent for a fresh code and needs no 04/SS ([meInvoice on TT91](https://www.meinvoice.vn/tin-tuc/48237/thong-tu-91-2026-tt-btc/); [MISA ASP](https://asp.misa.vn/tt/kien-thuc/hoa-don-thay-the-co-can-gui-mau-04-khong)). **Cash-register invoices and invoices for registrable assets must be replaced, not adjusted** ([MAN](https://ketoan.man.net.vn/05-thay-doi-quan-trong-khi-dieu-chinh-hoa-don-dien-tu/)).

TT91 also changes the details of the NĐ 70 regime the business-scenario research described. One adjustment or replacement may cover several erroneous invoices issued in the same month to the same buyer, with bảng kê **01/BK-ĐCTT**. The invoices must share goods, unit price, VAT rate and now also **quantity**, a condition NĐ 70 lacked ([meInvoice on TT91](https://www.meinvoice.vn/tin-tuc/48237/thong-tu-91-2026-tt-btc/)). A written agreement with organisational and household buyers is still required before correcting. Individuals can instead be notified or informed by a website notice. There are new exemptions for data sent by summary or transaction-detail tables and for sales on e-commerce platforms. Once a correction method is chosen for an error, later corrections of that error must use the same method ([MAN](https://ketoan.man.net.vn/05-thay-doi-quan-trong-khi-dieu-chinh-hoa-don-dien-tu/)). Adjustments are declared by the seller in the period issued and by the buyer in the period received. TT91 dropped the NĐ 70/TT 32 rule that errors in mandatory content go back to the original invoice's period, and EY advises checking VAT law on this point ([EY](https://www.ey.com/vi_vn/technical/tax/tax-and-law-updates/nghi-dinh-254-2026-nd-cp-ve-hoa-don-dien-tu-va-chung-tu-dien-tu)). Invoices issued under NĐ 123, NĐ 70 or TT 32 are corrected under the new rules ([MISA on TT91](https://amis.misa.vn/279873/thong-tu-91-2026-tt-btc/)). In the XML, the related-invoice type is 1 for those invoices and 5 for NĐ 254 invoices.

In the XML, an adjustment sets TCHDon = 2 and carries only the delta lines: **increases are positive and decreases negative**. A replacement sets TCHDon = 1 and carries the full corrected invoice. Batched corrections use messages 208 (coded) or 210 (uncoded). Each correction carries an Annex XVII reason code. One secondary source claims uncoded invoices can only be adjusted, never replaced ([MAN on TT91](https://thue.man.net.vn/thong-tu-91-2026/)). Until TT91 Art. 10 is read, treat that claim as unverified and drive the choice from the provider capability matrix. The correction module therefore needs these parts: a document-linking model (one-to-many, with the 01/BK-ĐCTT list), an agreement attachment, a reason-code field, a rule that forces replacement for M-series invoices, and no cancel button for any signed invoice.

## Conclusion

The 2026 reset turns compliance into a versioning problem rather than a feature list. At least four independent clocks now govern what a valid invoice looks like:

- the legal regime (NĐ 123/NĐ 70 before 01/07/2026, NĐ 254 after);
- the message version (2.1.0 until 31/12/2026, 2.1.1 after);
- the signature standard (DSig until 09/04/2027, XAdES after);
- the VAT rate (8% until 31/12/2026).

An app that hard-codes today's rules will be wrong within five months. One that resolves rules from the invoice date and the original invoice's date will survive the next amendment as well. Most of the XML and signing burden sits with the provider. The developer's highest-leverage decision is therefore which providers to support, and the first check is whether each has already shipped QĐ 1233 / 2.1.1 and XAdES. Whether the app talks to them directly or through an aggregator such as SePay or Tingee is secondary.

The penalty design points to where engineering effort pays off most. Fines scale with invoice count, the tiers cap at 70–80M VND, and consumers are paid to report missing invoices. A single systemic timing error is therefore costlier than any individual data mistake. The strongest guardrail is structural: generate the invoice from the delivery note at handover, and the export invoice from customs clearance. Never let an invoice wait for the cash receipt or the month-end close. Pair that with a correction module that cannot cancel, and the app covers the two failure modes the sources report most often among Vietnamese accountants: invoicing late and cancelling instead of adjusting.
